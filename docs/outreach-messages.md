# WhatsApp outreach messages

_Drafts for the founding offer (11 October 2026). Campaign messages are typed into the outreach tool (`apps/outreach`, Campaigns), not stored in the code, so paste these in there. The tool fills in `{name}` and `{city}` from each contact (nothing else). Founding prices end on 22 October 2026 at 23:59, Zimbabwe time: after that, use the "After 22 October" versions._

## First message

> Hi {name}, this is Kin from StayZim in Mutare. We build lodges in {city} their own website, free: your rooms, photos, a map and a Book on WhatsApp button, on your own yourlodge.co.zw (free too).
>
> You only pay once you've seen it and love it: from $15 a month. That's our founding price, and it's yours for life if you join before 22 October.
>
> Shall I build your lodge's site for you to look at? Just send me 3 photos.

## Replies

**"How much is it?"**

> Three plans, at founding prices until 22 October (locked for life):
> - Starter $15/month (normally $25): your website, a free .co.zw domain, rooms, gallery, map and Book on WhatsApp buttons.
> - Growth $30/month (normally $40): all of that, plus a booking calendar on your site and visitor stats.
> - Pro $50/month (normally $75): all of Growth, plus we look after your Booking.com and Airbnb photos and text, and priority WhatsApp support.
>
> No commission on any booking. Pay for a year and get 2 months free.

**"What do I have to do?"**

> Send me your lodge's name, your WhatsApp number and 3 photos. I'll build your site and send you the link. Love it? Launch it. Not? Pay nothing.
>
> Or make it yourself in about a minute: stayzim.co.zw/create

**"Do I have to leave Booking.com?"**

> No, keep your listing. Your own site turns guests who already know you (repeat guests, Facebook followers, people a friend sent) into direct bookings, with no 15 to 20% commission.

**"Can I cancel?"**

> Any time, on WhatsApp. Your site stays live until the end of the month you paid for.

**Two days before the deadline (20 October)**

> Hi {name}, a quick one: founding prices end on Thursday (22 October). Join before then and you keep $15, $30 or $50 a month for life, instead of $25, $40 or $75. Want me to build your site today?
